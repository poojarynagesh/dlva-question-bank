Instructions printed on the paper
  • This is a closed-book examination for 30 marks.
  • MCQs have at least one correct option unless explicitly mentioned.
  • For MCQs, partial marking will be given for incomplete but correct answers.
  • For short questions, make sure your reasoning is clear and to the point.

How MCQs are marked on this site

The paper promises partial credit for "incomplete but correct" answers. This site uses the natural reading of that rule:

$$\text{marks}=\begin{cases}0 & \text{if any selected option is wrong},\\[2pt] \text{pts}\times\dfrac{\#\text{correct options selected}}{\#\text{correct options}} & \text{otherwise.}\end{cases}$$
What this means for you

Picking a wrong option costs the whole question, while leaving out a right one only costs a fraction. Tick only what you can justify. If you are sure of two options and unsure of a third, the two sure ones are the safer answer.

Strategy

MCQs (≈ 35 min)

  • Read every option as an independent true/false statement.
  • Look for absolute words — "always", "never", "guaranteed", "any" — they are often the false ones.
  • For numbers, write the formula first: $F(K^2C_{in}+1)$, $(W-K+2P)/S+1$, $2N-2$, $\sum T_i\alpha_ic_i$.
  • "All of the above" / "None of the above": check each option individually first.

Short questions (≈ 20 min)

  • Answer in 3–5 sentences: the mechanism, the consequence, and a fix if one is asked for.
  • One equation often replaces a paragraph: $W_2(W_1x)=(W_2W_1)x$; $\sigma(\mathrm{ReLU}(z))\ge0.5$.
  • Name the concept — "exposure bias", "mode collapse", "degradation problem" — the examiner looks for it.

Long question (≈ 30 min)

  • Parts build on each other; do part (a) carefully, then reuse it.
  • For dynamics, draw the map against the diagonal $h_{t+1}=h_t$; above the diagonal $h$ grows, below it shrinks.
  • For gradients, write the per-step factor (e.g. $w\,\sigma'(\cdot)$) and ask whether it is above or below 1.

Before the exam

  • Do the solved midterm without looking, then check.
  • Take two mock papers under the timer.
  • Re-do every question you got partly wrong; the explanation is the revision.
